# Changelog

## [2.0.0] — 2026-11-24

### Added

- Pairwise Duels with per-track Elo, ties, skips and keyboard controls.
- An accessible tier board, album insights and rank comparison with Kendall's tau.
- Compressed read-only links, JSON interchange and a downloadable image of the top five.
- English and Brazilian Portuguese, offline support and an introductory guide.

### Changed

- Original generative artwork and locally served fonts replace the photography and external fonts.
- Listening links open a search on YouTube Music using the track and artist.
- Existing personal scores and favorites migrate to versioned browser storage.

### Fixed

- Album and track ordering follow the editorial album sequence.
- The catalog determines album counts, song counts, empty summaries and tier ranges.
- Rapid score edits, browser history, search updates and notice timers retain their intended state.
- Asset URLs resolve at both the domain root and the repository subpath.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-24

### Added

- Album and overall rankings, song details, filters, favorites and personal ratings.
