## Fotografia e capas / Photography and artwork

A fotografia promocional de _folklore_, de **Beth Garrabrant**, mostra Taylor Swift entre as árvores
de uma floresta. A versão 1 obteve o arquivo via [Vogue México][vogue]. As três capas vieram do
catálogo Apple Music. Os arquivos WebP são versões redimensionadas dessas quatro imagens,
recuperadas do [commit da primeira edição][v1]. As músicas usam a capa de seu respectivo álbum.

The original hero photograph by **Beth Garrabrant** shows Taylor Swift standing among forest trees.
Vogue México reproduced the photograph. The three album covers came from Apple Music. The WebP files
are resized copies of these four assets from the first edition. Album titles, photography and cover
artwork belong to their respective owners. The code license applies to code only.
